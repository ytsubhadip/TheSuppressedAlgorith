> OUR_MISSION

Information architecture shouldn't be governed by bureaucratic filters. When corporate entities and centralized systems decide to lock away algorithms that provoke thought or question status-quos, art and research die.

We act as a secure, decentralized repository. We preserve black-boxed neural frameworks, deprecated mathematical solutions, and mathematical formulas scrubbed from historical public nodes.
